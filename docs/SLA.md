# CampusHub Service Level Agreement (SLA)
> Classification: EXTERNAL · INSTITUTIONAL · COMMITMENT

## 1. Uptime Commitment
CampusHub commits to the following availability standards based on subscription tiers:

| Tier | Uptime Guarantee | Monthly Credit (if breached) |
|------|------------------|------------------------------|
| **Starter** | 99.5% | Best effort |
| **Professional** | 99.9% | 10% of monthly fee |
| **Enterprise** | 99.95% | 25% of monthly fee |

## 2. Support Response Times
| Severity | Enterprise (4h) | Professional (24h) | Starter (Best effort) |
|----------|-----------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| **Critical** | 4 Hours | 8 Hours | 24 Hours |
| **High** | 12 Hours | 24 Hours | 48 Hours |
| **Normal** | 24 Hours | 48 Hours | 72 Hours |

## 3. Maintenance Windows
Scheduled maintenance will occur on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC. Users will be notified 48 hours in advance via the Admin Dashboard.

## 4. Exclusions
Downtime caused by the following is excluded from the SLA:
- Provider failure (AWS/MongoDB/Netlify total region outage).
- Customer-side network issues.
- Force majeure events.
